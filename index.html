Lehigh Valley Gamer Symphony Orchestra

A casual, come-as-you-are community orchestra for video game & geek culture music. If you want to play, we want you here.

info.exe _ □ ✕

Lehigh Valley Gamer Symphony Orchestra (LVGSO) is a community orchestra based in the Lehigh Valley, PA, performing music from video games and geek culture. We're inspired by groups like NJGSO and GPGSO — bringing that same love of game music to our own corner of Pennsylvania.

We're casual and supportive first, musical second. No cutthroat auditions, no chair-ranking stress — just a room where every musician grows together. Got five pianists in one rehearsal? Everyone plays, on digital pianos, covering different voices in the score. There's always a part for you.

LVGSO is a 501(c)(3) non-profit. We keep rehearsals and concerts accessible to musicians and audiences alike, relying on donations and community support to keep the music (and the tuba section) running.
