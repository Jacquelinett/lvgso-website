Jacqueline Ott-Tran is an aspiring pianist based in the Lehigh Valley. Growing up in an Asian family, she spent the majority of her life chasing the traditional Asian-parent dream: chasing the money, only to discover her love for piano in late 2024 and now doing everything in her power to chase that dream instead.

She is currently a part-time pianist for a local church and a piano technician apprentice. Jacqueline studies classical piano, music theory, and jazz, but her main goal with piano is to arrange and play solo anime and video game music (and whatever her current AuDHD hyperfixation happens to be).

Outside of piano, Jacqueline is a full-time software engineer. She also casually plays drums to fill in for LVGSO, worships Cinnamoroll, plays DDR, lifts weights, and occasionally draws. She is the main contact person if you have any questions about the orchestra—and, given her profession, she also designed the orchestra's website!
