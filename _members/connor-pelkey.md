Connor Pelkey is a guitarist, bassist and vocalist with 20 years of experience as a performer. He got his start on saxophone before discovering the bass guitar after dissecting the battle music from Final Fantasy IV, or Final Fantasy II if you're old and from the US.

From 2012 to 2022 he performed as bass player for the band Waydown Wailers based in Syracuse, NY. However, in between gigs with that group he contributed heavily to MAGFest as the audio engineer for the live stream as well as recording 5 albums of original music and a Mega Man X/Mega Man Zero concept record for http://ocremix.org - since 2022 he's been wanting to perform music live again.

Aside from his musical interests and his dayjob as a scheduling supervisor, Connor likes lifting weights and going to Lehigh Valley Phantoms hockey games with his wife.
