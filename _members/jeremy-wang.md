Jeremy is an avid and occasionally degenerate gamer who also plays video game music. Having played classical piano from a young age, he now draws influence from jazz, funk, and fusion for his music. In addition to the LVGSO, he plays in the Pixel Groove Guild, a local VGM band based in Maryland. His favorite band is Casiopea, and spends way too much time on the computer.
