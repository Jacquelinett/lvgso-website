Lauren Olivola is a Pennsylvania-based horn player who freelances in concert bands and orchestras across New Jersey and Eastern Pennsylvania. She has a variety of avenues covered in her career within music education, including teaching in public and private school, being a clinician, assisting on staff with marching band, field adjudication during the marching season and being an educational representative for Elefante Music in New Providence, New Jersey. In her role at Elefante Music, she has a personal mission to help music teachers serve their student horn players more confidently in their music programs.

Lauren was a founding member of the New Jersey Gamer Symphony Orchestra, formerly called Montclair Gamer Symphony Orchestra, back in 2014. She also served as an assistant conductor while a member there. She is proud to see how far NJGSO has come since relocating to the Lehigh Valley in 2024. Lauren is now proud to be a founding member of the Lehigh Valley Gamer Symphony Orchestra, supporting the orchestra's endeavors as a musician and advertising to other video game orchestras in the area.
